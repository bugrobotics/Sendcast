Where the rock is best within your range today, across all Colorado-area crags. Every hour gets a score from friction temperature, humidity (higher is worse), sun on the rock, wind and rain, and whether the rock has dried since the last storm. The day's score is the best three daylight hours, or your chosen time slot when you set one.

MapCircle shows your range. Dots are colored by score; the top five are numbered.

Hourly forecasts from Open-Meteo, requested at each crag's own elevation (US runs use the ~3 km HRRR blend). Drive times and road miles come from the OSRM router on OpenStreetMap roads. They don't include traffic or the last stretch on dirt roads, and the radius filters by road miles. Area list, coordinates and style tags come from Mountain Project, 8a.nu and climber forums; names link to each area's Mountain Project page. Coordinates are approximate; always check access, closures and raptor seasons before you go.
